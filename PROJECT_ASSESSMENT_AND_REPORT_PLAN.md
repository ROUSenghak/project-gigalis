# Part 1 — Internal project assessment

## 1.1 What the project actually does

One sentence: it turns 1.6 million official BOAMP notices into a cohort of 3 800 awarded digital procurement episodes in Grand Ouest, constructs an **observable successor** event because BOAMP carries no legal-renewal label, estimates how long an episode takes to show such a successor, and adds a supervised text classifier that recovers a business technology segmentation the CPV codes do not carry.

The five workstreams and their status:

| Workstream | Delivered | Guide deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Data acquisition, standardisation, episode reconstruction, cohort, data quality | Yes | L1 |
| Successor linkage + evaluation on a 120-anchor regional reference | Yes | (prerequisite for L3) |
| Survival analysis: KM, log-rank, Cox, PH, temporal validation, parametric, 4 robustness families | Yes | L3, minus individual prediction |
| NLP technology taxonomy: 500 annotated notices, 11 classes, TF-IDF + logistic regression, gated downstream use | Yes | L2 (single-annotator) |
| Trend and change-point: quarterly series, PELT, ADF/KPSS, HMM, multiplicity correction | Yes | L4 |
| Methodological report + reproducible pipeline | Yes | L5, L6 |
| Causal inference (Gigalis membership effect) | Design outline only, no estimate | optional 4th dimension |

## 1.4 Strongest components — lead the report with these

1. **The paired text-vs-CPV comparison.** Out-of-fold macro-F1 0.7442 against 0.4731 for the best CPV/descriptor benchmark on *identical* folds with an *identical* search budget; paired difference 0.2711, 95 % family-bootstrap CI [0.2009, 0.3403], excluding zero. The bootstrap resamples *procurement families*, which is the correct unit given the leakage structure. This is the cleanest quantitative result in the project and it directly answers the guide's text-signal problem.
2. **CPV-35 as a robust comparative finding.** HR 1.553 (p = 3.8 × 10⁻⁴) against CPV-32; survives the four linkage arms, the ±0.05 borderline band (1.780), template-risk re-censoring (1.541) and detectability adjustment (1.512). Four independent perturbations, same direction.
3. **The robustness architecture itself.** Four event-definition arms (296 → 1 332 events), a borderline band, a template-risk re-censoring, and a detectability sensitivity. The resulting statement — *absolute levels are linkage-sensitive, several relative comparisons are stable* — is a scientific result, not a caveat.
4. **The retained threshold discipline.** 0.70 was retained after inspection of the designated reference evidence; `QUALITY_EVIDENCE.md` publishes the sweep showing that 0.60 has better recall without promoting it post hoc. The sweep is therefore an internal validation and sensitivity display, not a fully held-out assessment.
5. **Leakage control in the NLP layer.** Procurement families (episode ∪ character-cosine ≥ 0.80), 0 of 459 families spanning a fold, vectoriser inside the pipeline, nested inner CV, temporal split with boundary families forced into *training*.
6. **Gate A costs significance rather than buying it** (technology log-rank p 0.000119 → 0.0363 when the residual classes are excluded), and it was kept. This is the strongest available evidence that the analysis was not tuned toward a result.
7. **Negative results published, not buried.** Out-of-time Cox C-index 0.479 — below chance — is stated as the result, with an explicit refusal to retune.

## 1.5 Weaknesses — ranked by what they could do to a conclusion

**A. The evaluation reference is LLM-generated, not human ground truth (most important).**
The 120-anchor regional reference was labelled by a single LLM research pass over BOAMP notices, official URLs and wider public sources on 2026-08-11, then spot-checked on a subset by the project owner — not verified anchor by anchor. The 60-pair "independent" review is likewise model-assisted (`review_provenance.json` records the model identity explicitly and states it is *not* independent human validation). Consequence: precision 0.875 rests on **8 accepted links**, CI [0.529, 0.978], and the completed diagnostic gives 0.700 conservatively (14/20), below the project's own 0.80 target. **The report must never call this validated accuracy.** It is honestly documented everywhere in the repository; the report must keep it that way.

**B. Candidate-surfacing independence does not hold, and cannot be recovered.**
The rule that selected the ~25 candidates exported per anchor for review (from pools up to 3 258) was not recorded anywhere. All 16 retrievable locked-split successors sit in the top 13 by the very text score being evaluated. So **recall (0.389) and the 0.913 candidate-generation ceiling are not fully independent of the score they bound**; precision is unaffected. Disclosed in four documents. Keep the distinction sharp: labels are independent, the candidate list is not.

**C. Event rate 14.3 % against the guide's 40–60 % planning figure.**
Not a miss to hide — a consequence of two deliberate choices: a precision-first threshold, and an *observable successor in BOAMP* definition rather than a legal-renewal one. The report should state this explicitly and early, because a reader who remembers the guide will otherwise read 14.3 % as failure.

**D. Detectability confound in the framework finding.**
Candidate-pool size is the largest linked-vs-censored imbalance (SMD +0.470 on the log scale) — larger than any contract-level variable — because `M_B` takes a maximum over the block and the maximum of more draws is larger. Adding log(pool size) attenuates the framework HR from 1.751 to 1.617 (≈14 % of the log HR) while CPV-35 barely moves. The framework association is real but partly observability.

**E. Cox proportional hazards is violated for three covariates**, severely for `award_year_centered` (statistic 70.7, p = 4 × 10⁻¹⁷ — and award year is structurally confounded with follow-up length). Coefficients are time-averaged descriptive associations only. Stated in the reports; must be equally clear in the final one.

**F. No inter-annotator agreement on the technology corpus.** Single pass, no annotator identifier, so no Cohen's κ. The guide's L2 design (two annotators) is not met. `AI` has 7 annotated notices and is declared unevaluable. The learning curve is still rising at n = 500 (macro-F1 0.434 → 0.747 across fractions, train ≈ 0.99 throughout: high variance, not underfitting), so a second annotation round is worth more than a bigger model.

**G. Individual prediction is not achieved.** Out-of-time C-index 0.479 (2022–2024) / 0.518 (2022–2025). The guide's L3 asked for per-contract 12/24-month predictions and a top-20 table for the Gigalis portfolio. No active Gigalis portfolio was available, and the model does not discriminate out of time. What exists instead is a **cohort-level watch logic** (segment × age conditional probabilities from KM). That substitution is defensible and must be argued, not glossed.

**H. The cohort is broader than "digital procurement" sounds.** Any-CPV-code rule at episode level: 1 176 of 3 800 (30.9 %) have a *main* CPV outside 32/35/48/72 — multi-lot tenders entering on one digital lot. Measured and documented; needs one sentence in the report so no reader over-reads "3 800 digital procurements".

**I. Trend series are short and noisy.** 43 quarters, low counts. No segment slope survives Holm/BH correction; CPV-48 is a nominal signal only (raw p = 0.032, Holm 0.159). The 3-state HMM on 43 quarterly differences is the lowest-value component per unit of complexity — worth keeping *only* as a descriptive complement, clearly labelled non-forecast.
